## Timetable

The workshop runs from Monday September 21 to Friday September 25, 2026.

* (K) Keynote talks are 45 minutes each, followed by 15 minutes of discussion.
* (C) Contributed talks are 30 minutes each, followed by 15 minutes of discussion.
* (S) Short talks are 23 minutes each, followed by 7 minutes of discussion.
* (F) Flash talks are 12 minutes each, followed by 3 minutes of discussion.

Long lunch breaks are planned to foster interactions between participants. All participants are welcome to present a poster.
