# An Ontology for Theoretical Chemistry

**Speaker:** Mario Wolter
**Affiliation:** Institute of Physical and Theoretical Chemistry, TU Braunschweig, Gaußstraße 17, 38106 Brau, nschweig, Germany

**Type:** Workshop / Discussion

---

### Abstract

This workshop introduces participants to ontologies and their role in standardizing theoretic\
al chemistry. I will present ongoing work on the Basis Set Exchange Ontology\cite{BSEO}, whic\
h uses data from the Basis Set Exchange\cite{BSE}. The focus on the BSEO as a starting point \
 serves as a learning platform for developing ontologies with modern tools and workflows and \
also develop as usable ontology on basis sets.

Workshop goals:
* Introduction to ontologies: concepts, tools, and best practices for theoretical chemistry.
* Presentation of current BSEO development
* Hands-on guidance on how to contribute to BSEO
* Collaborative design of how other areas of theoretical chemistry can be modeled in ontology form, using BSEO as a template.

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to navigate ontology tools, understand the current BSEO structure, and actively contribute to extending it to further domains of the
oretical chemistry.
